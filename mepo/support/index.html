Common questions

Where are my memories and reminders stored?

Only on your device. Mepo has no accounts and no external servers, so we can't see or recover your data. You can export a full backup file at any time from Settings → Export & Backup All Data.

How does natural-language capture work?

Type or dictate your thought naturally—for example, "Amazing carbonara at Circolo, pasta 5/5" or "Pick up dry cleaning tomorrow at 5pm high priority". On devices with Apple Intelligence, Mepo extracts the title, category, place, star ratings, due date and priority on device while always keeping your original note.

How do location reminders work?

When adding or editing a reminder in Day to Day, enable a location alert and choose a place from your saved memories, your current location or place search. You can choose to be notified when you arrive or when you leave, and adjust the distance radius.

How do I open a saved place in Apple Maps?

Tap the mini-map preview on any memory card or reminder detail view to open that location directly in Apple Maps for directions and place details. You can also switch the Memories tab between list and map view using the top toolbar toggle.

How do I back up, restore or export my data?

Tap the gear button in the top-left corner to open Settings. Choose Export & Backup All Data to save a complete JSON backup (including photos, ratings and coordinates) or a readable Markdown export. Choose Import & Restore Backup to restore a JSON backup file without duplicating existing items.

How do I remove the sample memories and reminders?

If you chose "Explore with Sample Data" on first launch, open Settings (the gear icon) and tap "Start Fresh (Remove Sample Data)". Any memories or reminders you created yourself will be kept safe.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Mepo removes its memories and reminders from your device. Use Settings → Export & Backup All Data first if you want to save a backup copy.
