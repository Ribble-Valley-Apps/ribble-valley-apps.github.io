Mepo · Memories & Reminders

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

The short version: Mepo does not collect, store on external servers, sell or share any of your personal data. All of your memories, photos, places, ratings and reminders stay on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Who we are

Mepo is developed by Ribble Valley Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains how Mepo handles your information on iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad.

Information we collect

None. Mepo does not collect any personal information, usage data, diagnostics, location history, contacts, photos or device identifiers on our servers, and does not send your content to us or to any third party.

Your memories and reminders

Your memories, notes, star ratings, attached photos, saved locations and reminders are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData storage and shared only with Mepo's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets on the same device. We cannot see, access or recover your data.

On supported devices, Mepo uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence) to organise natural-language entries into titles, categories, places and reminder schedules. This processing happens entirely on your device.

If you use Apple's iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your device's backup may include Mepo's local data. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you under your device settings, not by us.

Device permissions

Mepo asks for optional device permissions only when you use features that need them:

  • Location (When In Use): Used on your device to tag a memory with your current location, show memories near you, search for places via Apple MapKit, and trigger arrival or departure reminder alerts. Your location is never transmitted to us.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: Used only when you tap the microphone button to dictate a memory or reminder by voice using Apple's speech recognition service.
  • Photos: Used only when you choose a photo to attach to a memory or save a shared memory card image to your photo library.
  • Notifications: Used on your device to deliver scheduled time and location alerts for your reminders.

Sharing and backups

When you share a memory card or export a backup file (JSON or Markdown), Mepo hands the image, text or file to the iOS share sheet or Files picker. You choose where it goes. Once shared or exported, that file is handled by the destination you picked and its own privacy policy.

No accounts, tracking or advertising

  • Mepo does not require an account or sign-in.
  • Mepo contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Mepo does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children's privacy

Mepo is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the UK GDPR and EU GDPR give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete. You can edit or delete any memory or reminder inside the app, or remove all of Mepo's data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If Mepo's handling of information ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
