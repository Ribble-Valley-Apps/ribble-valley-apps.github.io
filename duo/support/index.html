Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need two devices or an internet connection to play?

No! Ready Player Duo is designed specifically for head-to-head play on a single shared screen. Place the device flat between you or prop it up. All games run 100% offline without Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular data.

Which games are included?

Six games are included out of the box: Sunk (naval fleet combat), Checkers (classic draughts), Suspects (deduction & guessing), Hang (letter & word guessing), Crack (codebreaking duel), and Tip of the Tongue (forbidden word party game).

How does the screen layout work?

On iPhone and iPad, the screen divides into two seats facing each other across the table, rotating controls so each player sees their board right-side up. On folding devices like iPhone Duo, games unfold side-by-side or stand in tent mode.

How does Tonight’s Score work?

Ready Player Duo tracks wins between Player 1 and Player 2 across sessions. You can view or reset the score anytime by tapping the trophy or settings icon on the main menu.

How do I customize player names and colors?

Tap the gear / settings icon on the game box lid to change Player 1 and Player 2 names and avatar colors.
