Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

The short version: Ready Player Duo does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertisements, and no tracking. Everything stays strictly on your device.

Who we are

Ready Player Duo is developed by Ribble Valley Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains how Ready Player Duo handles your information across iPhone and iPad.

Information we collect

None. Ready Player Duo does not collect any personal information, gameplay data, analytics, crash logs, device identifiers, location, or contacts. It makes zero network requests to our servers or third parties.

Local game data

Your custom player names, player colors, game sound/haptic toggles, and "Tonight's Score" are stored locally on your device using standard system storage. We have no access to this data, and it is never transmitted over the internet.

No accounts, tracking, or advertising

  • Ready Player Duo requires no account, sign-in, or registration.
  • It contains no tracking SDKs, analytics, or advertising frameworks.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It works completely offline without an internet connection.

Children's privacy

Ready Player Duo is designed for family and casual play across all ages. Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under privacy laws such as the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, you have rights regarding your personal data. Because Ready Player Duo holds no personal data about you, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. Deleting the app from your device clears all locally stored scores and preferences.

Changes to this policy

If our handling of data ever changes, this page and its effective date will be updated before changes take effect.
