Common questions

Where are my notebooks stored?

Only on your device. Folio has no accounts and no cloud sync, so we can't see or recover your notebooks. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, those backups include Folio's data.

How do I switch between typing and drawing?

Tap a page to start typing, or switch to the pen to sketch. The toolbar lets you swap between Text and Pen at any time, choose pen, pencil, marker or eraser, and pick a stroke size and ink colour.

How do I turn, rename or delete pages?

Pull a page corner to turn the page. To rename, reorganize or delete a page, long-press its row on the Contents page.

How do I change a notebook's cover or paper?

Tap the sliders button on the cover to open Customize Cover, where you can choose one of six cover colours, one of four paper styles (graph, dot grid, ruled or blank) and the lettering on the front cover.

How do I export a PDF?

Use the share button on the notebook shelf to export a notebook as a PDF, then choose where to save or send it from the share sheet.

Does Folio work on iPhones that don't fold?

Yes. Folio works on standard iPhones with a single page at a time. On iPhone Duo you also get a full two-page spread when unfolded.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Folio removes its notebooks from your device. Export anything you want to keep as a PDF first, or make sure you have a recent device backup.
