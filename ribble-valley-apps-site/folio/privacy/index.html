Folio · Field Memo & Sketchbook

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

The short version: Folio does not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal data. Everything you write and draw stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Who we are

Folio is developed by Ribble Valley Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains how Folio handles your information on iPhone and iPhone Duo.

Information we collect

None. Folio does not collect any personal information, usage data, diagnostics, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers, and does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

Your notebooks

Your notebooks, pages, typed notes, sketches and cover settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see, access or recover them.

If you use Apple's iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your device's backup may include Folio's data. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you under your device settings, not by us.

PDF export and sharing

When you export a notebook or spread as a PDF, Folio hands the file to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes, such as Files, Mail or another app. Once shared, that file is handled by the destination you picked and its own privacy policy.

No accounts, tracking or advertising

  • Folio does not require an account or sign-in.
  • Folio contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.
  • Folio does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Folio works fully offline and makes no network requests for your content.

Children's privacy

Folio is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the UK GDPR and EU GDPR give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete. You can remove all of Folio's data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If Folio's handling of information ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
