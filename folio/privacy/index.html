Folio · Field Memo & Sketchbook

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

The short version: Folio does not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal data. Everything you write, draw and attach stays on your device, and, if you choose, in your own iCloud so it can appear on your other devices. We never see it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Who we are

Folio is developed by Ribble Valley Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains how Folio handles your information on iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad.

Information we collect

None. Folio does not collect any personal information, usage data, diagnostics, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers, and does not send any data to us or to anyone else. Photos you attach to a page are copied from the picker you choose them in; Folio cannot see the rest of your photo library.

Your notebooks

Your notebooks, pages, typed notes, sketches, attached photos and cover settings are stored on your device. We cannot see, access or recover them.

If you use Apple's iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your device's backup may include Folio's data. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you under your device settings, not by us.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Folio can keep your notebooks, drawings and photos in step across your own devices (for example your iPhone and iPad) using Apple's CloudKit. This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time from your notebook shelf in the app.

  • Your data is stored in the private area of your own iCloud account, which only you can access. It is not stored on our servers, and we have no access to it.
  • It is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and counts towards your iCloud storage.
  • Turning sync off stops Folio sending or receiving changes. It does not delete notes already in your iCloud storage or on your devices. You can remove Folio's iCloud data in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.
  • If you sign out of iCloud or switch to a different Apple Account, Folio pauses sync and keeps the notebooks on your device. It never sends them to a different account unless you turn sync back on.

PDF export and sharing

When you export a notebook or spread as a PDF, Folio hands the file to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes, such as Files, Mail or another app. Once shared, that file is handled by the destination you picked and its own privacy policy.

No accounts, tracking or advertising

  • Folio does not require an account or sign-in.
  • Folio contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.
  • Folio does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Folio works fully offline. The only network use is iCloud sync, if it is on, which talks to Apple's iCloud and not to us.

Children's privacy

Folio is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the UK GDPR and EU GDPR give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete. You can remove all of Folio's data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

If Folio's handling of information ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
